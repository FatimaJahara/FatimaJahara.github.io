I work on reasoning in multimodal and multilingual language models — how models break under distribution shift, where their reasoning takes shortcuts, and how to align their output with human preference and ethics.

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Rutgers, advised by Dr. Sharon Levy, and a student researcher with the FAIR Alignment team at Meta, where I previously interned. My recent work spans implicit bias in LLM reasoning, dialect robustness in text-to-image generation, and benchmarks for evaluating vision-language metrics. Earlier, I built NLP datasets and models for Bangla, a low-resource language, at the CUET NLP Lab.

Before my Ph.D., I was a Senior Assessment Developer in generative AI, NLP, and deep learning at Workera. I am always happy to talk about multimodal evaluation, multilingual fairness, or research in low-resource settings — reach out.

Projects

LLM Prompting for Socially Cognizant Robotics Repo

A structured study of AI-assisted coding and research workflows: prompting LLM agents to build a web app, a PyTorch MNIST classifier, and a Q-learning maze solver, then stress-testing them through iterative debugging — including a case where the agent described a correct maze-generation algorithm but produced an unreachable goal, and corrected itself once shown the rendered maze. Also contrasts manual, AI-only, and human–AI collaborative literature review on time, depth, and citation reliability. Extends to a U-Net segmentation experiment on the Oxford-IIIT Pet dataset.

Walker2D SAC Reinforcement Learning Agent Repo

A Soft Actor-Critic agent trained on the Walker2D continuous-control benchmark — off-policy, entropy-regularized deep RL for a high-dimensional locomotion task.

University Enrollment Log System Repo

A web-based system for managing student–course assignments, with interactive D3.js visualizations over a Django backend. Built during a software development traineeship at Brain Station 23.

Fire Extinguisher

Simulation-based navigation with four bots on a grid of blocked cells and dynamically spreading fire; Bot 4 adds enhanced decision-making for pathfinding and survival.

FoodFond

Restaurant recommendation engine using latent-factor collaborative filtering over ratings, reviews, and personalized preferences.
